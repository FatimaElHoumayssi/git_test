Crispy Garlic Cheese Cauliflower

This is a delicious and easy-to-make recipe that's perfect for a quick and satisfying meal.

Roasted cauliflower slices with garlicky crunch and melted cheese topping.

Ingredients: (Serves 4)

  • 1 big head cauliflower, cut into 1-inch thick slices
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tsp chopped fresh parsley

Instructions:

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Brush cauliflower slices with oil on both sides.
  3. Sprinkle garlic, salt, and pepper over them.
  4. Put on a lined baking sheet. Roast 22 minutes, flip once.
  5. Top with cheese. Roast 3 more minutes till melted.
  6. Add parsley and serve warm.

Tip:


Broil 1 minute at end for extra golden edges; store leftovers 2 days in fridge.
